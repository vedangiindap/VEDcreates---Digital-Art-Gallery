The Artist

VEDcreates – creative space

I'm Vedangi Indap, the creator and visual designer behind VEDcreates. For me, art is an open exploration—an ongoing masterclass in capturing culture, geometry, and modern visual aesthetics across completely different canvases.

I love traveling between centuries with my styles, transitioning seamlessly from the complex tribal structures of traditional Indian folk-arts like Warli, Pichwai, and storytelling Madhubani paintings to structural geometric line-work found in detailed Mandala configurations.

At the same time, I translate these age-old rhythms into the contemporary landscape using digital applications like Ibis Paint X to create expressive portraits, minimalist wallpapers, and vibrant vector layouts. Art, for me, doesn't sit inside a rigid box—it is an endless adventure to link imagination with genuine human connection.

Let’s build beautiful visuals together.

Open for creative collaborations, client commissions, digital concept design, and artistic cross-over projects.
